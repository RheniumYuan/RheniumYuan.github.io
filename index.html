Computational psychiatry & mental health

Understanding minds,
through computational science.

My name is Yuan Minghao (袁明昊). I am a Ph.D. student in the Depression and Anxiety Computational Neuroscience Lab (DACN Lab), mentored by Dr. Yujia Peng at Peking University. My research focuses on computational psychiatry and mental health, with particular interests in computational modeling, network analysis, and quantitative approaches to understanding human behavior and mental health. I am also interested in research methods on complex psychological systems, including causal inference, computational modeling, network science, behavioral data science, and large language model (LLM).

I welcome collaboration opportunities on research related to mental health, computational psychiatry, quantitative methods, LLM, and related interdisciplinary topics.

Projects

psychHypergraph

psychHypergraph implements information-theoretic hypergraph analysis for psychometric data, based on Marinazzo et al. (2024) and Possenti et al. (2026). The package provides tools for constructing psychometric hypergraphs, identifying synergistic and redundant higher-order interactions, centrality analysis, clustering, and visualization.
